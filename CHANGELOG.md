# Changelog

The instrument's versions, newest first, by what each changed on the wire: commands, responses, report keys, error numbers and stream records, as [docs/scpi.md](docs/scpi.md) defines them, and the behavior a host can observe. The version is `firmware/version.txt`; the host package (`host/pyproject.toml`, `kleidos_meter.__version__`), its simulator and the firmware's host-test harness carry the same number, and `tests/firmware/test_versioning.py` holds all of them and the top entry of this file together.

## The version rule

- **Minor** (`0.4.0` → `0.5.0`): a new command, a new report key, a new error number, a new stream record, or a behavior a host can observe changes (a new refusal, a new bound). A new key is appended at the end of its report: a key keeps its place.
- **Patch** (`0.5.0` → `0.5.1`): a fix that changes nothing a host can see on the wire.
- **Major** (`0.x` → `1.0.0`): a command, a key or a record removed.
- **A changed meaning is not allowed.** A key is never renamed or given another unit, and a command never comes to mean something else: a new meaning is a new key or a new command (`AGENTS.md`, "The protocol is a contract").
- Every version that reaches `main` is tagged `v<version>`, annotated and signed, on the commit that sets it in `firmware/version.txt`, and gets its entry here in the same commit. `SYSTem:INFormation?` names the running build by `fw=` and, from 0.5.0, by `elf=`, the first 8 bytes of its ELF SHA-256.

The tags `v0.2.0`, `v0.3.0` and `v0.4.0` were set on 2026-11-20 on the commits that set those versions; from 0.5.0 each version is tagged when it lands.

## 0.18.0 (2026-11-27)

The meter's wiring is data the instrument stores and the panel changes, and the panel's chart draws the arithmetic mean. The GPIOs of the INA228's SDA, SCL and ALERT and of the two relays are a pin map, held to the board's pin table (`firmware/board/pins.toml`, generated into the firmware and the panel), applied only once the INA228 answers on it and put back by itself when it does not; the INA228's averaging and shunt conversion time are a measurement setting per profile, off by default; the panel draws each pixel column's mean over a faint band of its minimum to maximum, with display smoothing that never alters what was recorded, and changes the pin map on a drawing of the board. The images built on the way reported `fw=0.17.0` and are told apart by `elf=`; the release's bench acceptance, with its ELF digest, is in `docs/misra.md`, "The run of record".

**What the Kleidos QA harness must know** (`docs/scpi.md`, "The pin map" and "The averaging override").

- **Keys are appended, none moved or renamed.** `SYSTem:INFormation?` and `SYSTem:HEALth?` end with `pins_src pins_table`, so `elf=` is no longer the last token of `SYSTem:INFormation?`; `ROUTe:STATe?` ends with `relay1_gpio relay1_active relay2_gpio relay2_active`; `SENSe:PROFile:REPort?` with `avg_state vbusct_us`; `FETCh:HEADer?` with `avg_state pins_src`. `MeterLink` parses reports by key and reads none of them; its fakes in `qa/tests/tools/meter_sim.py` answer the earlier keys, which is still a valid answer to it. `*IDN?` does not change: its four fields are IEEE 488.2's, and every parser splits on them.
- **New commands**: `SYSTem:PINS?`, `:PENDing?`, `:STAGe`, `:ADDRess`, `:CLOCk`, `:CLEar`, `:APPLy`, `:DEFault`, `:RESult?`; `SENSe:AVERage[:STATe]` and its query, and the settings `SENSe:AVERage:COUNt <n>` and `SENSe:CURRent:APERture <seconds>` beside their queries. The averaging override is off on every boot and after `*RST` (`SENSe:AVERage?` `0`, `avg_state=off`), the profiles' own `ADC_CONFIG`, as before: a run that never sends them measures as on 0.17.0. A harness that reads `period_us`, `avg` and `vshct_us` from the capture header, rather than assuming a profile's, stays right when a person has turned one on; `*RST` turns every one off.
- **A new error, `117` "Pin map reverted"**, queued at boot when a stored pin map's bus did not answer (FW-04), and by `SYSTem:PINS:APPLy` for a map that was put back (FW-05); and `*RST` returns the I2C clock to the pin map's boot clock, 400 kHz by the default map, as before.

- No key, command, record or error number is removed or given another meaning; `*IDN?` is unchanged.

## 0.17.0 (2026-11-24)

The firmware allocates nothing from `app_main()`'s first line on, and the meter has a panel on the PC, over the cable. The images built on the way reported `fw=0.16.6` and are told apart by `elf=`, and the counts an entry below states are those of its own work package's image; the release's, with its bench acceptance and its ELF digest, are in `docs/misra.md`, "The run of record".

**What the Kleidos QA harness must know.**

- **After the first flash of 0.17.0 over an earlier image, `kleidos-meter calibration restore`.** The calibration moved from NVS to the firmware's own record log (FW-16, below) and nothing reads NVS any more, so the first boot reports `q=uncal` and `kleidos-meter calibration verify` answers `working_copy_lost` until the registry's copy is written back. The nulls are session state and are taken again, as after any flash. A later flash of 0.17.0 or a newer image keeps the log, unless it erases the whole flash.
- **`DIAGnostic:HEAP?` ends with four new keys** (`pre_main_allocs pre_main_frees allocs_since_main frees_since_main`, FW-07); the earlier keys keep their places, `maint_allocs`, `maint_frees` and `free_spiram` read 0 and `armed` always 1. `MeterLink` reads none of them.
- **Every parameter past the most a command takes is `-108`** (FW-30), where most commands ignored it; `MeterLink` sends no such unit. `-363` is queued once per message an overrun discards (FW-12).
- **The host tools hold the port.** `kleidos-meter`, in every form, opens the CH343 port exclusively, as `MeterLink` already does: whichever comes second is refused at once. While `kleidos-meter serve` runs, a QA run reaches the meter through its passthrough (`--scpi-passthrough`, `socket://127.0.0.1:5025`) with a client that opens a pyserial URL; `MeterLink` opens a device node only, so for it `serve` is stopped first.
- **Hold a long dump to its CRC-32 and ask again.** The line has no flow control, and on 2026-11-24 a full ring's `TRACe:DATA? SCALed` (36.6 MB) reached the host damaged, or short, the first time it was asked for in both sessions of the acceptance that reached it, whole the second time (`docs/testing.md`, "A damaged dump is the line's"); `RAW` is a tenth of the size, and is the one the host tools read.
- No key, command, record or error number is removed or given another meaning. `INITiate` no longer has its `-221` `stream too fast`, which no profile could reach (FW-27).

**On the host: the panel and its process** (`docs/panel.md`, `docs/http-api.md`, `host/README.md`).

- **`kleidos-meter serve`**, the panel on the PC over the cable: one process, the standard library and pyserial, the only opener of the port while it runs. It serves the panel's page (plain ES modules, no third-party JavaScript, its chart on its own Canvas) and a JSON HTTP API on 127.0.0.1:8040, the live samples, marks, status and errors as Server-Sent Events, and a schema of every setting, sequence and route, with what each route takes and answers (`GET /api/schema`). It starts by taking a snapshot of the capture-shaping settings and writes back what it changed on the way out; it never sends `*RST` and never changes `FORMat`.
- **One button per measurement sequence** (`HEALTH`, `NULL_RELAY`, `NULL_LEAD`, `STATE`, `SLEEP`, `TRACE`, `BURST`, `COLD_BOOT`, `CAL_VERIFY`, `CAL_RESTORE`), each with a cleanup that runs whatever happened and leaves the relays on the shunt. The shunt is shown with the registry's value and uncertainty, and an override is taken only with a stated tolerance or uncertainty, under a "Not quotable" banner until the registry's copy is restored.
- **The exclusive open.** Every host tool opens the port with an `flock` (`SerialTransport(exclusive=True)`, the default) and a second opener is refused with `PortBusyError`, told which `serve` holds it; until now two openers each lost some of the other's lines. `SerialTransport` also opens a pyserial URL.
- **The SCPI passthrough** (`serve --scpi-passthrough [PORT]`): one TCP client at a time on 127.0.0.1, byte for byte in both directions, the panel a monitor meanwhile and the settings `serve` changed written back before the line is handed over.
- **Recordings**: every capture `serve` owns is a run directory (`stream.txt`, `trace.raw` with its CRC-32 checked, `meta.json`), exported as text, CSV or JSON, and refused inside a git work tree; `kleidos-meter capture` records one with no panel. `protocol.decode_dump()` decodes a `RAW` or `SCALed` ring dump to columns, a `RAW` one to the integers the `SCALed` one prints.
- **The passthrough refuses a web page.** A cross-site `fetch()` to `http://127.0.0.1:5025/` from any page reaches the passthrough as an HTTP request whose body lines are program messages, past every guard of the panel's HTTP API; one with `ROUT:LEAD OPEN,FORC` in its body opened the DUT's lead. The client's bytes now reach the instrument a whole line at a time, each looked at first, and a client that writes a line of an HTTP request is dropped there with nothing from it sent (`docs/panel.md`).
- **The page keeps its events, stays quiet and takes only what serve takes.** An answer of `/api/events` that is not a stream (`serve`'s 503 at its 8 streams, or while it closes) closed the page's `EventSource` for good, and the page froze while saying it reconnected; it now opens a new one after 1 s, doubling to 15 s. The chart's readout, a live region rewritten each second while it follows a capture, is announced only after a pan, a zoom or the crosshair, not for the whole capture. The run and mark label fields took 200 and 64 characters where `serve` takes 80 and 15 (and no space or `,;="'` in a mark): their limits now come from `/api/schema` (`max_length`, `pattern` of each argument), so a mark is not refused after the click.
- **A header ends where the firmware ends it.** The firmware ends a unit's header at a space, a tab, a carriage return or a newline (`chars::isSpace`), and runs `SENS:PROF<TAB>SLEEP` as it runs `SENS:PROF SLEEP`; the simulator, `serve`'s executor (an acquisition's time-out, the settings a message changes, which `serve` hands back on the way out and before the passthrough) and its schema split on a space alone, so the simulator answered -113 and `serve` took a tab-separated `MEAS?` for a 5 s query and a changed profile for none. All of them now split as the firmware does (`sim_table.split_header()`), and the conformance suite asks both with a tab.
- **The simulator's null reports are the firmware's** (DO-05): `CALibration:ZERO:RESult?` carries `profile`, `n_expect` and `samples` while the window is open, `n` counted at the profile's period, and the relay tokens of the circuit the window was opened in, as `cmdZeroResult` writes them; it answered `n_expect=74 path=none` and `n=74 path=<path>` before. Nothing changes on the wire. `host/tests/test_scpi_doc.py` sends [docs/scpi.md](docs/scpi.md)'s worked session to the simulator and holds every answer to the bench's keys.
- **A message with a quote left open is refused unsent** (the review of 2026-11-24, P2). Both the firmware and `serve` split a message only outside quotes, so the `;:SYST:ERR:ALL?` `serve` appends after an open quote was quoted with it: the message got no answer to match, and every request stalled for the class's time-out before the line was resynchronized; `GET /api/query` let any page do it. `serve` now refuses such a message on every route, as it refuses one the instrument would discard.
- **What the instrument queues on its own is an event, never the next request's error** (the review of 2026-11-24, P1). Every message `serve` sends ends with `;:SYST:ERR:ALL?`, so a hold's release (`113` to `115`), relays a reset released (`116`) or an overrun's `-363`, queued since the last poll, came back in whichever message was next: a read of the settings, the relays or the health answered 409 with an error it had not caused, and the notice reached neither the event stream nor the running capture's `meta.json`. The executor now takes those codes out of a caller's reply and gives them to the listeners as `DeviceErrors`.
- **A run is exported only once it has ended** (the review of 2026-11-24, P3). An export asked for while its run was recording was made from the partial `stream.txt` (or before a ring run's `trace.raw` existed) and kept, and every later export of the run served that short file. `GET /api/runs/<run>/export` now answers 409 (`recording`) for the run serve is still writing, `/api/runs` says which one that is (`recording`), the page offers its exports only once it has ended, and each export is written under a name of its own before it replaces the kept one, so two requests at once no longer write one file.
- **`POST /api/scpi` says which of its lines ran when a later one fails** (the review of 2026-11-24, P4). The route runs its messages one after another and answered only the failure when one after the first timed out (504), found the queue full (503, `Retry-After: 1`), found the line down or handed over, although the lines before it had already changed the instrument; a client that honored `Retry-After` sent the whole body again and ran them twice. It now answers 200 with the `replies` of the lines that ran, `failed` (`message`, `error`, `detail`) and `unsent`, and no `Retry-After`; the console writes the same. A failure of the first line, before anything ran, answers as before.
- **A second Ctrl-C no longer cuts `serve`'s hand-back** (the review of 2026-11-24, P5). `serve` put the default handlers back as soon as the first signal stopped it, so a second Ctrl-C during a cleanup that can last minutes raised `KeyboardInterrupt` in the middle of it, and a second `SIGTERM` killed the process: a window it opened stayed open and the settings it changed were not written back. Its handler now stays until the port is released: a second signal says the hand-back is still running, a third abandons it, says so and exits 1.

- **The watchdog is the firmware's own, on the timer ESP-IDF's task watchdog used** (FW-11): `CONFIG_ESP_TASK_WDT_EN` is off, and Timer Group 0's main watchdog timer is set through its low-level layer for a system reset 5 s after its last feed, fed only once the console, the sampler and Core 0's idle task have all checked in (`docs/architecture.md` §5.1). Nothing is allocated: `allocs_since_main` reads 2 fewer (the task watchdog's two subscriptions) and `pre_main_allocs` 4 fewer (its object, Core 0's idle entry and its interrupt's two records). A starved task now resets the chip instead of panicking; the reset reason is the same (`SYSTem:HEALth?` `reset=task_wdt`), and `DIAGnostic:WATChdog?` and `DIAGnostic:WATChdog:TEST` keep every key and meaning. A console that spins without blocking is now caught too, through Core 0's idle task.
- **A full ring's `TRACe:DATA?` no longer resets the instrument** (FW-11). The dump formats its rows twice, first only to count the block's bytes, and that pass neither checked in with the watchdog nor blocked, so for a full ring (about 5.6 s on the meter) it starved the console's check-in and Core 0's idle task past the 5 s timeout; the same held under the task watchdog since 0.14.0. The counting pass now checks in, and every check-in of the console blocks for one tick (a millisecond in a hundred).
- **The console's UART0 on its registers, without ESP-IDF's UART driver** (FW-12): `allocs_since_main` reads 15 fewer once the boot has settled (35, with `frees_since_main` 8), the driver's object, receive ring buffer, event queue, mutexes and interrupt records gone. The port is set through its low-level layer, its interrupt routed to a free line of Core 0 with nothing allocated, received bytes go to a static ring of 1,024 bytes with the 128-byte FIFO behind it, as before, and the transmit is polled (`docs/architecture.md` §4.2). A line that takes nothing for 10 ms now ends the write it holds, counted as `dropped=`, where the driver would have waited for ever. Rate, frame, pins and every response are as before.
- **A receive overrun queues one `-363` per message it discards** (FW-12). A dump looks for a loss at every row, and until now queued one `-363` for each FIFO overflow a long burst caused while it ran; and the mark that the next message lost bytes was cleared when the message that found it (the dump's own) ended, so the tail of the burst ran as a message of its own, or was refused again as too long. The loss now marks the message it fell in until that message ends, and is reported once.
- **The INA228's bus is the firmware's own I2C master, without ESP-IDF's I2C driver** (FW-14): `allocs_since_main` reads 12 fewer once the boot has settled (23, with `frees_since_main` 8), the driver's bus object, semaphores, queue, handle slot, device object and interrupt records gone, and a change of the bus clock (`SYSTem:COMMunicate:I2C:FREQuency`, and `*RST` when the clock is not 400 kHz) allocates and frees nothing, where it removed and allocated the device handle again (2 allocations and 2 frees, counted as `maint_`). ## 0.16.0 (2026-11-21)

- **The hold is the teardown, and an open capture no longer suspends it.** Before, the 120 s hold waited for every open capture, so a host that died with a capture or a stream open and the lead open (`ROUTe:LEAD OPEN,FORCe`, or under a lead cycle's order) left the DUT without its battery, or the null path standing, until somebody came. - **A real `*TST?`.** Besides the bring-up's three bits (1 INA228, 2 sampling engine, 4 relay inputs) it now reads back the INA228's identity and `SHUNT_CAL` (8), its trim memory `MEMSTAT` (16), probes the `ALE` wire with STATE's pacing probe (32), the relay pads (64), the stored calibration (128) and the tasks' stacks (256). It reads the device, so it is refused while a window is open (`-221`, `112`; it answered before).
- New `SYSTem:HEALth?`: `reset uptime_us elf fw stack_console stack_sampler allocs_after_init frees_after_init hold_released errors`, the reset reason as ESP-IDF names it among them.
- **`STATus:QUEStionable` gains bit 2, time** (the open or last capture's pace fell back, a read failed, or `pace_unmet`), **and bit 9, data lost** (`dropped=` above 0); new `STATus:QUEStionable:PTRansition`, `:NTRansition` and the same for `STATus:OPERation`, with SCPI's defaults (every rise, no fall), which `STATus:PRESet` restores.
- New error **113, "Null path released"**, queued when the hold releases a null path by itself.
- **`CONFigure` and `MEASure` of the voltage, the power or the energy select `STATE`** when the live profile converts no bus voltage: `MEASure:VOLTage?` after a boot on `TRACE` answers instead of queuing `-230`. `READ?` and `FETCh?` configure nothing and are unchanged.
- The host's `kleidos-meter calibration restore` without `--yes` and with no terminal says so and exits 2, where it died on an `EOFError`; `Meter.self_test()` and `Meter.health()` are new.

## 0.11.0 (2026-11-20)

- **A level trigger with pre-trigger on the ring profiles.** `TRIGger:SOURce LEVel` with `TRIGger:LEVel`, `TRIGger:SLOPe` and `TRIGger:PRETrigger`: the ring runs circular from `INITiate` until the current crosses the level from its other side, keeps the pre-trigger samples, records the rest of the ring after the crossing and ends the capture by itself. `SYSTem:STATus?` appends `trig=armed|fired|-`; `FETCh:STATistics?` appends `trig_n trig_t_us`; `FETCh:HEADer?` appends `trig_source trig_level_na trig_slope trig_pre limit`. A level on a stream or a statistics-only capture is refused (`-221`). `TRIGger:SOURce IMMediate` was the only source before, and is still the default.
- **Indices in a dump are the capture's.** After a wrap the ring's oldest sample is index `first=` (on the opening row), and `T n= t=` marks the trigger; `<from>` in `TRACe:DATA?` is a place among the held samples. An untriggered dump's rows are the ones they were.
- **The dump carries a CRC-32.** `METER_DUMP_END` becomes `METER_DUMP_END crc32=<8 hex> bytes=<n>`, the IEEE CRC-32 of every byte of the block before it; `Meter.dump()` raises on a mismatch.
- **Marks in conversion order.** On a streamed capture a `TRACe:MARK` or `LIVE` relay mark travels the stream queue behind the samples already in it, so it is never written before a sample of a lower index (it could overtake them).
- New `SENSe:CURRent:THReshold:MARK ON|OFF`: a streamed capture writes `thr_rise` and `thr_fall` mark records at the threshold's crossings, behind their samples, at most 1,024 a capture; `FETCh:STATistics?` appends `thr_marks`.
- New `SENSe:CAPTure:LIMit ON|OFF`: `INITiate` ends a stream or a statistics-only capture by itself after `SAMPle:COUNt` samples.
- **`*OPC` waits** for a window that ends by itself (a ring, a triggered ring, a limited capture, a null) and sets the ESR's bit 0 when it has; with none running it sets it at once, as before. `*OPC?` and `*WAI` stay immediate (`docs/scpi.md`, "Deviations"). `*CLS` and `*RST` forget a pending `*OPC`.

## 0.10.0 (2026-11-20)

- **The statistics the uncertainty model needs**, appended to `FETCh:STATistics?`: `sd_na` (the sample standard deviation of the current, from every sample, in 128-bit integer arithmetic), `max_n` and `max_t_us` (where the maximum fell), `vmin_uv vmax_uv pmin_uw pmax_uw` (the DUT's voltage and power extremes on a bus profile), `mean_charge_na` and `charge_us` (the CHARGE pair's shunt-corrected mean current over the span between its own two reads), `thr_na above_n above_us rises` (the threshold below) and `batches batch_samples`. Every figure is counted in the sampling loop, so a sample the stream dropped is still in it.
- New `FETCh:STATistics:BATCh?`: the last capture's batch means, 32 to 63 complete batches of equal size for a capture of 32 samples or more, from which a host estimates the effective sample size.
- New `SENSe:CAPTure DATA|STATistics`: a statistics-only capture, no stream and no ring, on any profile for any duration (TRACE's rate for hours). Its header and `SYSTem:STATus?` say `sink=stats`; `*RST` sets `DATA`.
- New `SENSe:CURRent:THReshold <amps>|OFF` and `SENSe:CURRent:THReshold:HYSTeresis <amps>`: a level the captures count rises and time above against, with hysteresis. `*RST` sets `OFF` and 0.
- `FETCh:VOLTage?`, `:POWer?` and `:TEMPerature?` are means over every sample the sampler took (before, over the samples that reached the console), and `FETCh:ENERgy?` is the mean power times the capture's `dur_us` (before, times the samples the console saw, so a dropped sample shortened it). Their meaning is unchanged; on a capture with no drop the figures are the same.
- `CALibration:ZERO:RESult?`'s `sd_pv` keeps the fraction of a count: the variance was truncated to whole counts squared before its root, which read a null's deviation low (to 0 below one count, by up to 1.4 % at six counts); it is scaled first now, as the new `sd_na` is.
- After `*RST` closes a running `STATE` or `SLEEP` capture, `FETCh:VOLTage?` answers that window's mean (it queued `-230`): the statistics `*RST` keeps now carry their own sums.

## 0.9.0 (2026-11-20)

- **`STATE` and `SLEEP` are paced by the `ALE` pin.** The pin is held low from a conversion's end until `DIAG_ALRT` is read, in either `ALATCH` mode (bench, 2026-11-20, `docs/firmware-design.md` §3.5), so the sampler now reads `DIAG_ALRT` after every edge and its probe at `INITiate` does the same: their headers say `pace=alert pace_edges=<7..9> pace_expect=8` where every capture before said `pace=timer pace_edges=1`. `ALATCH = 1` stays (the clip detector), and on `STATE` that per-sample read now carries a latched `SHNTOL`/`SHNTUL` into `flags=` too (`diag_cfg=0xC001 alatch=1` unchanged).
- **`TRACE` and `BURST` stay on the host timer and no longer probe**: their headers say `pace=timer pace_edges=0 pace_expect=0` (`pace_expect` was 8).
- **An alert-paced sample is stamped at its edge**: `t=` in the stream is when the conversion-ready edge woke the sampler, the conversion's end, rather than when its reads finished.
- `FETCh:STATistics?` appends `stale_edges=`: edges of an alert-paced capture whose `DIAG_ALRT` read found no conversion ready. They take no sample; `samples / (samples + stale_edges)` is the fresh-sample ratio. Always 0 on a timer-paced capture.
- `SENSe:CURRent:RANGe` waits out the conversion period the device is running, not the selected profile's: before the first capture the device converts at its reset word's 3,156 µs, and the 280 µs wait of `TRACE` left `SENSe:CURRent:RANGe:REPort?` reporting `cnvrf=0` after most switches (`discard_us=3156` now where it said `280`).

## 0.7.0 (2026-11-20)

## 0.6.1 (2026-11-20)

## 0.6.0 (2026-11-20)

- An acquisition (`MEASure`, `READ`, `CALibration:SHUNt`) is bounded: `SAMPle:COUNt` times the profile's period may not exceed 600 s, else `-222` and nothing starts (10^6 `SLEEP` samples were 18.8 hours of a deaf console). `SAMPle:COUNt` itself still takes 1..1000000.
- `ABORt` ends a running acquisition within a sample period: the acquisition queues `-230` (`aborted after <n> of <count> samples`) and answers nothing; the `ABORt` unit is consumed and everything sent meanwhile runs after, in order.
- A stop the sampler task does not acknowledge within 500 ms (`ABORt`, an acquisition) queues `107` and closes the window with no result; a new window is refused with `107` until the task acknowledges. The loop's statistics cross to the console by value with that acknowledgement.
- New flag **C** in `docs/scpi.md`: `SENSe:PROFile`, `SENSe:CURRent:RANGe[:UPPer]`, `SENSe:CURRent:RANGe:INDex`, `CONFigure:CURRent`, `SENSe:CHARge?`, `SENSe:CHARge:RESet`, `DIAGnostic:ALERt?` and `DIAGnostic:REGister?` are refused (`-221`, `112`) while a window that ended by itself is not yet closed. Before, `SENSe:CHARge:RESet` on a filled ring zeroed the accumulator between the window's two boundary reads.
- A byte that is not printable ASCII or a tab discards its program message with `-101`, naming the byte in hex; it is never stored or echoed, and an error's detail never carries one.
- Input lost to a full receive ring while the console is busy queues `-363` and discards the message the loss fell in.
- `*RST` re-creates the I2C device only when the clock is not 400 kHz already.
