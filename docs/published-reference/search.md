# Search

The search at the top of every page finds a document, a topic, a class, a namespace, a file or a member by its name or by the words of its description, with the closest match first. The `/` key and Ctrl-K (Command-K on a Mac) open it from anywhere; the arrow keys move through what it finds, Enter opens one and Escape closes it.

It runs in the browser, over two lists this site publishes beside its pages, and asks nothing of a server. Without a script, these pages lead to the same names:

- <a href="index.html">Overview</a>: every document of the meter, from using it to its firmware's design.
- <a href="topics.html">Topics</a>: the parts of the firmware, from its entry point down to the bit work.
- <a href="annotated.html">Classes</a>: every class and record, by the namespace it is in.
- <a href="functions.html">Names</a>: the members of every class and record, from A to Z.
- <a href="globals.html">File-level names</a>: the macros and the names outside every namespace, from A to Z.
- <a href="namespaces.html">Namespaces</a>: every namespace, inside the one it is in.

The browser's own search (Ctrl-F, Command-F) finds a word on the page that is open.
