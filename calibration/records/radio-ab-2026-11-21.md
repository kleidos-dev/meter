# 2026-11-21 — NW-00, the radio A/B: a radio on the meter moves its measurement, so the meter stays serial-only

One unattended night, from 15:37:35 to 20:40:15 (CET), on instrument `KM-0000000000`, configuration `mjkdz-r100-relay2click`, the DUT (the T-Deck, about 88 mA) on the shunt. The question was the owner's rule for a Wi-Fi meter: "las medidas no deben verse afectadas, sino descartaremos wifi y solo funcionará vía serie" (the measurements must not be affected; otherwise Wi-Fi is dropped and the meter works over serial only). Every judged metric, in every Wi-Fi state a user would run, had to be indistinguishable from the same image with the radio never started, judged against that reference's own block-to-block spread; no TX-power, scheduling or quieting mitigation was to be tried as a way to pass.

**Verdict: FAIL.** In the worst case, C4 (the meter as access point, one client receiving a 200 kB/s stream and polling status at 1 Hz), five measures moved and the confirmation, on fresh blocks, found them moved again: the null, the floor's standard deviation and its Allan deviation, and the spectra of the floor and of the DUT. The night ended there, as its fast-fail rule says, and the owner ruled on 2026-11-21 that the meter stays serial-only (firmware 0.16.6, `CHANGELOG.md`). The TX-power sweep run after it, which asks whether a lower transmit power would have passed, is [radio-tx-sweep-2026-11-21.md](radio-tx-sweep-2026-11-21.md): it would not.

The states, each a block of the same null-path window and `STATE` stream: **C0**, the `c0` image; **C1**, the `spike` image with Wi-Fi never started (the reference of every comparison); **C4**, the `spike` image as access point with the client streaming. The client was the bench's M5StickC Plus 1.1 host harness in its Wi-Fi mode. The Relay 2 Click's RL1 switched a 100 Ω load (`load_g_ppm`) within each block; it switched in every block.

The schedule: a 20-minute warm-up and six blocks on C0, the flash to `spike`, an A/A of C1 (n_0 = 24 pseudo-pairs), then C4 in ABAB against C1 (n = 20 pairs), with C6 (station) and the idle states (C2, C3, C5) to follow only if C4 was clean. 149 blocks were measured and none was spoiled. Each metric's difference D_x (C4 minus the flanking C1) is judged against a 99 % interval from the A/A, corrected for the overlap of the pairs; a spectrum against T99, the 99th percentile of the A/A's own resampled spectra; an excursion is confirmed on fresh blocks of the same state before it counts. With the measured covariance of the seven interval metrics, the complete two-stage rule gives a **0.81 %** chance of a confirmed false detection over the night (the spectra not included: their T99 comes from the A/A itself).

The confirmation, on 20 fresh pairs, found the same side again for each of the five: the null at −0.152 µV, the floor's standard deviation at +0.0213 (log, about +2.2 %), its Allan deviation at +0.105 (log). The DUT's mean went the other way (+29.0 µA) and is not among the detected.

In the radio blocks: `dropped=0 read_err=0`, no `-363` and no garbled line (any of them would have been an effect by itself). The pacing kept the INA228's own oscillator (`pace_err_ppm` not moved): the IRAM work of 0.16.1 holds with the radio up.

Causes, recorded and not judged: the die temperature on − off +0.0119 ± 0.0024 °C (mean) and +0.0071 ± 0.0093 °C (peak to peak). The 3V3 rail was not measured by the spike. The radio: transmit power 20 dBm, the client's RSSI −52 .. −47 dBm, the stream 200 kB/s over all 40 C4 blocks, the lowest internal free heap 136,836 bytes.
