# 2026-11-19 — as found at the move to SCPI, and one range-0 null

The state of the instrument's calibration when it left the Kleidos firmware repository and its firmware went from 0.1.0 (console protocol) to 0.2.0 (SCPI). Read on the bench, over the CH343 line.

## As found (firmware 0.1.0, `CAL?`)

```text
shunt_uohm=100000 shunt_measured=1 off_pv_r0=-951928 off_pv_r1=-919552 nulled_r0=1 nulled_r1=1 off_die_mc_r0=23609 off_die_mc_r1=23625 vbus_side=source cal_tag=R100_0.10ohm_1pct_1028
```

- Shunt: the nominal 100 000 µΩ, flagged measured; the tag says the 1 % part of 2026-10-28. The two-point calibration against the VPG reference has not been run (`../README.md`).
- Nulls: −0.95 µV on range 0 and −0.92 µV on range 1, both taken at about 23.6 °C die.
- `VBUS` sensed on the source side.

## The flash kept it

Firmware 0.2.0 reads the same NVS blob: `CALibration:DATA?` after the flash returned the line above unchanged.

## One range-0 null

To exercise `CALibration:ZERO` on the new firmware, a 3 s null was taken on range 0, `SLEEP`, with the DUT's lead opened by K1 (`ROUTe:LEAD OPEN,FORCe`, `path=none`; the T-Deck stayed on USB, so nothing crossed the shunt):

```text
running=0 range=0 offset_pv=482954 sd_pv=312500 over_s=3 die_mc=25914 n=44 k1=lead:open k2=bypass:open k3=spare:off k4=spare:off path=none check=- stored=1
```

It replaced the range-0 null: +0.48 µV at 25.9 °C, 1.43 µV (14 µA at 0.100 Ω) from the one before it. That spread is the thermal EMF in the sense loop the bench has seen since 2026-10-31 (nulls between −0.9 and −2.5 µV over 80 min), not the ADC; the QA harness brackets every capture with its own nulls, so no stored figure rests on this one.
